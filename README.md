# EVENTLOGS

An automated register of fatalities and injuries reported by public news
sources worldwide, extracted, deduplicated, and published as flat facts
with no personal information. It's a register, not a news site.

![EVENTLOG Screenshot](images/screenshot.png)

## What is EVENTLOGS

Each record is a single line: country, cause, location, severity, and a
link back to the source article. No headlines, no narrative, no ranking of
which events matter more. The site is deliberately presented as a stream
of log lines, the same way a server logs what happens inside a system —
because that's what this is meant to be: a flat, factual record of events,
not a story about them.

## What you won't find here

Every record is stripped of anything that could identify a person: no
names, no ages, no occupations, no addresses. Locations are limited to
city or region level. There are no photos, no death-toll headlines, and
nothing designed to be dramatic or shareable. This isn't just an editorial
choice — it's enforced before a record is ever published.

## Sources and corrections

Events are gathered from public news sources across many languages and
regions, and each record stays attributed to where it came from. If you
spot something inaccurate, or believe a listing shouldn't be here, please
reach out — see the contact link below.
